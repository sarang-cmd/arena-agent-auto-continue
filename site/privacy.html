Privacy

Applies to the browser extension and this website. Last updated 7 November 2026.

This extension exists to send the word "continue". That job does not require collecting anything about you, so it does not. This page describes the small amount of data that stays on your own computer, and confirms what never leaves it.

What the extension stores

One record in your browser's local extension storage. It contains your settings (whether auto-send is on, the delay, the message text, the safety cap, and the guard switches) and a counter of how many messages have been sent in this session. You can clear it by removing the extension, and nothing is written to disk outside that storage area.

What the extension can see

The extension is granted access to pages on arena.ai only, because that is where the banner appears. On those pages it reads text in order to answer one question: is the tool-call limit banner on screen? It also reads whether the message box is empty before typing into it.

Text from your conversations is not copied, stored, logged, or transmitted. It is inspected in place and discarded immediately.

What never happens

  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension contains no network requests.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, or fingerprinting.
  • No account, sign-in, licence key, or payment.
  • No remote code. Everything that runs is in the files you installed.
  • No reading of other tabs, browsing history, bookmarks, or downloads.

Permissions, in plain language

  • storage keeps your settings and the send counter on your machine.
  • activeTab lets settings ask the current arena.ai tab for its status while the settings view is open.
  • Host access to arena.ai is what allows the script to see the banner and fill the message box.

This website

These pages are static files with no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party embeds. The only network requests they make are for the pages' own files. Fonts are taken from your operating system, so nothing calls out to a font service.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted in the release notes.
